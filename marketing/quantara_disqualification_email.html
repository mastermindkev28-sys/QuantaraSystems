Honestly? Most people on this list won't clear our bar for a strategy session.

We'd rather tell you that up front than waste twenty minutes of your time and ours. Quantara doesn't run strategy sessions to "see if there's a fit" — we run them because the fit was already screened for. So before you book anything, read the four lines below. They'll tell you, in about thirty seconds, whether this is worth your time.

You will likely be turned away if —

— You're looking for a signal service, a "guru," or a get-rich timeline. We model systems; we don't sell hope.
— You need an answer about an allocation inside the next two weeks. Our process moves at the pace of due diligence, not urgency.
— You haven't sat through a drawdown before and aren't sure how you'd react to one. That's not a judgment — it's just not what this session is for.
— You can't explain, in one sentence, why systematic exposure interests you more than discretionary trading. If you can, you've already done more homework than most applicants.

If none of those four lines describe you — if you read that list and thought "obviously not, why are you even asking" — then you're exactly who the strategy session is built for, and you should book it today. We'd rather spend our twenty minutes with the person who already cleared the bar than spend it convincing someone to clear it.
